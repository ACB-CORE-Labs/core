## ADR-0060 — CORRECTION Acknowledgement Carries the Corrected-Topic Lemma

**Audit ID (if a numbering collision):** — | **Family (if phased):** —

### 1. Content summary

- **Decision made:** extend `pack_grounded_correction_surface` (ADR-0053) to accept optional raw `text`, extract the first pack-resident topical lemma left-to-right (excluding the meta-cognition lemma `correction`/`correct` and the fillers `be`/`have`), and weave it into the acknowledgement surface so a correction like "Actually, truth requires evidence" surfaces `truth`, not just `correction`. Degrades byte-identically to the ADR-0053 template when no topical lemma is found or `text` is `None`.
- **Alternatives explicitly rejected:** using `intent.subject` (ADR-0049's head-noun extraction) instead of raw-text parsing — rejected because `intent.subject` for CORRECTION preserves the post-marker tail (`", truth requires evidence"`) rather than a single head noun, and extending ADR-0049's normalization to the CORRECTION intent, or re-parsing `intent.subject` at the composer, would be equivalent or greater work than parsing the raw text directly.
- **Artifacts the ADR claims will exist:**
  - `chat/pack_grounding.py::_extract_correction_topic_lemma` helper
  - An optional `text` parameter on `pack_grounded_correction_surface`
  - A call-site change in `chat/runtime.py` passing `text` through (single line)
  - `tests/test_correction_topic_lemma.py` (14 tests)

### 1. Content summary
