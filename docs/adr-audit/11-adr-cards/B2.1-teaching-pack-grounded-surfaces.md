All seven ADRs date from a single day (2026-05-18) and continue the causal chain Batch 1's B6 card established: `chat/runtime.py:_maybe_pack_grounded_surface`'s own docstring ("ADR-0048 / ADR-0050 / ADR-0052 — three reviewed sources of cold-start grounding share this dispatcher") is confirmed still accurate at the current SHA, and every one of this zone's seven ADRs is a further branch, composer, or resolver layered onto that same dispatcher — not a parallel reimplementation. Read end to end, they form one architectural arc: ADR-0052 adds the teaching-corpus grounding source (CAUSE/VERIFICATION) as dispatcher branch three; ADR-0061 adds a fourth composer for PROCEDURE; ADR-0062 adds an opt-in depth-1 "chain-of-chains" variant of the teaching composer; ADR-0063 generalizes the *pack* lookup from one hardcoded pack id to an ordered resolver over N mounted packs (`chat/pack_resolver.py`, now resolving across at least 13 packs at the current SHA — far beyond its original two); ADR-0064 does the same generalization at the *teaching-corpus* layer (a `TEACHING_CORPORA` registry, now three specs, still one corpus per pack); ADR-0066 adds thread-level state (bounded FIFO of prior turns), an opt-in anaphora prefix, and two new intents (NARRATIVE, EXAMPLE) that read across the whole corpus registry; ADR-0067 closes the one gap ADR-0064 explicitly left open — chains whose subject and object resolve in *different* packs — with a distinct, narrower cross-pack chain schema and its own corpus file. ADR-0064 and ADR-0067 share a title ("Cross-pack teaching chains") but are **not** the same decision twice: ADR-0064 is "many single-pack corpora, one registry" (its own text: "each corpus is **1:1-bound to exactly one lexicon pack**"); ADR-0067 is "chains that literally span two packs in one entry" (its own text: "ADR-0064 ... deliberately kept each corpus 1:1 bound to a single ratified pack ... The articulation wall now lives in the *gap between* domains"). Neither ADR's front-matter carries a `Supersedes` field naming the other; both mechanisms (the corpus registry and the cross-pack chain loader) coexist live in code today. All seven ADRs' claimed artifacts were found live at the verified SHA and their dedicated test suites (202 tests across ten `test_*` files, run together) pass. The zone's one recurring liveness caveat continues Batch 1's `AA-B6-1` pattern: three of the zone's mechanisms (`composed_surface`, `thread_anaphora`, and by extension the later `transitive_surface`/`gloss_aware_cause` outside this zone) ship opt-in, default off, with `docs/specs/flag_register.md` explicitly grouping all four as sharing "no per-flag criterion recorded" for turning them on.
