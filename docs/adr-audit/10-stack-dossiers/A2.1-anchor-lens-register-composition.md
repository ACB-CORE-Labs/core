## 1. Stack-level claim

> **The claim:** CORE can produce *structurally different propositions* from different conceptual substrates, deterministically, by pivoting the composer onto a non-English lemma's `semantic_domains` atom *before* the proposition is built — an axis mathematically orthogonal to the register (presentation) axis, which varies only surface text after the proposition is fixed.

This stack **is** falsifiable, and the ADRs pre-register the criterion themselves — in three progressively weaker forms, which is the finding.

- **Pre-registered criterion (ADR-0073 §Phase L1.3 + §Phase L1.4, the strongest form):**
  1. `anchor_lens_lifts_proposition` — "same prompt × {unanchored, grc_logos_v1, he_logos_v1} produces **three different propositions** (surface differs **structurally, not just lexically**; trace_hash differs deliberately)."
  2. `surface_propositions_distinct_across_lenses` — "at least one prompt yields a **structurally-different proposition** across lenses (**not just marker variation**)."
  3. Orthogonality: `register-tour` ⇒ trace_hash CONSTANT across registers; `anchor-lens-tour` ⇒ trace_hash DISTINCT across lenses. "If either claim ever fails the seam is broken."
  4. ADR-0073 §Consequences, the capability statement: *"A Greek-anchored answer to 'what is knowledge?' does not rephrase the English answer; it answers from γνῶσις-vs-ἐπιστήμη as compound English phrasing"* — ADR-0073 §L1.3 gives the literal target string: `"Knowledge-as-experience is grounded in personal acquaintance, not abstract system."`

- **Measurement performed:** `python3 -m core.cli demo anchor-lens-tour --json` and `... demo orthogonality-tour`, both run at `cbfc8ccb` during this audit. Both exit `0`, all claims `True`. The grid:

  | prompt | lens | surface | trace_hash |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | What is knowledge? | `default_unanchored_v1` | `Knowledge is what a person knows from truth and evidence. pack-grounded (en_core_cognition_v1).` | `d4d87a7c…` |
  | What is knowledge? | `grc_logos_v1` | `Knowledge is what a person knows from truth and evidence. pack-grounded (en_core_cognition_v1) [lens(grc_logos_v1):systematic].` | `3c31f5d1…` |
  | What is knowledge? | `he_logos_v1` | *(byte-identical to unanchored)* | `d4d87a7c…` **(identical)** |
  | What is truth? | `default_unanchored_v1` | `Truth is what is true. pack-grounded (en_core_cognition_v1).` | `99820a63…` |
  | What is truth? | `grc_logos_v1` | *(byte-identical to unanchored)* | `99820a63…` **(identical)** |
  | What is truth? | `he_logos_v1` | `Truth is what is true. pack-grounded (en_core_cognition_v1) [lens(he_logos_v1):covenant-verity].` | `36df96c6…` |

- **Verdict: NO-GO on the stack's central claim, on the stack's own pre-registered criterion.**
  - Criterion 1 **fails**: 2 distinct propositions across 3 lenses, not 3, on both prompts. The repo's own test **hardcodes the failure as the expectation** — `tests/test_anchor_lens_lifts_proposition.py:110` `assert len(distinct) == 2  # grc differs; he matches base`.
  - Criterion 2 **fails**: the difference is *exactly* marker variation. The proposition — `"Knowledge is what a person knows from truth and evidence."` — is byte-identical under every lens. Nothing "structural" varies. The English gloss is emitted verbatim; the Greek/Hebrew substrate contributes a 30-character constant read out of the lens JSON.
  - Criterion 4 **fails**: no compound phrasing, no `Knowledge-as-experience`, no γνῶσις-vs-ἐπιστήμη distinction reaches the surface in any form.
  - Criterion 3 **passes vacuously** — see `AA-A2.1-2`/`AA-A2.1-17`: both tour claims are tautologies of the marker append, and the "orthogonality" is a consequence of which side of the `compute_trace_hash` cut each string edit lands on, not of any semantic independence.

  The shipped code pins a *third*, weaker criterion in place of both — `≥2 distinct trace hashes when engaged`, `≥1 prompt with >1 distinct surface` (`evals/anchor_lens_tour/run_tour.py:204–218`) — with no supersession note in any ADR. This is the same defect shape FA-1 ruled on: **a falsifiable claim, silently replaced in code by a weaker one that cannot fail, while the ADR still asserts the strong one.**

#### 3. Liveness / integration

- **Live serving path?** No, on defaults. `RuntimeConfig.anchor_lens_id` defaults to `None` → `AnchorLens.unanchored()` → `is_null_lens()` is `True` → `_resolve_anchor_lens_mode` returns at `chat/pack_grounding.py:338` before any work. The mechanism is reachable only via `core chat --anchor-lens <id>`, `RuntimeConfig(anchor_lens_id=…)`, the two demos, and `evals/frontier_compare`'s `axis_orthogonality` lane.
- Where reachable, the call chain is real and traced: `chat/runtime.py:2094` → `pack_grounded_surface(lemma, register=…, anchor_lens=self.anchor_lens)` → `build_pack_surface_candidate` → `_maybe_append_anchor_lens_annotation` → `_resolve_anchor_lens_mode` → `alignment.graph.load_alignment(<substrate cognition pack>)`.
- **Sabotage test (two levels, both decisive):**
  1. *Remove the substrate.* Replace the entire alignment-graph walk in `_resolve_anchor_lens_mode` with a hardcoded `{("grc_logos_v1","knowledge"): "systematic", ("he_logos_v1","truth"): "covenant-verity"}` dict. **Nothing observable changes.** Both tours still exit 0, all five orthogonality claims still hold, every `test_anchor_lens_*` still passes, telemetry is byte-identical. The alignment graph, the `semantic_domains` atoms, the two cognition packs, and ADR-0073a's twelve authored lemmas are, at the observable level, **decoration**.
  2. *Remove the annotation.* Stub `_maybe_append_anchor_lens_annotation` to `return surface`. Then `trace_hashes_distinct_across_lenses` and `surface_propositions_distinct_across_lenses` both fail. So the *only* thing carrying the substantive-axis claim is the string append — which confirms it is the marker, not the substrate, doing the work.
- **Liveness axis:** **wired-but-unreached** — fully wired end-to-end and demonstrably exercised by the demos, but off on every default configuration, and the part that is reached is the marker append rather than the substrate pivot.

1. **Direction of operation reversed.** ADR-0073 §"Why this composes against register, not replaces it": *"Anchor lens operates **before** the proposition is constructed — it picks which lemma's `semantic_domains` the composer should render, which changes the proposition itself."* The code constructs the full English proposition at `chat/pack_grounding.py:449–452`, then calls `_maybe_append_anchor_lens_annotation` at line 456. #### 7. Necessity / generality

1. **Necessity.** As built: not necessary. §3's sabotage test removes the substrate with no observable change. As *designed* (a real substrate pivot): the capability is genuinely desirable and not provided elsewhere — but the ADR's chosen mechanism is not the primitive that would deliver it.
2. **Reducibility.** The designed operation — "shift the rendered concept from the English coordinate toward the substrate's coordinate" — is **already an L0 primitive**: `algebra/rotor.py:285 word_transition_rotor(A, B)` composed with `algebra/rotor.py:85 rotor_power(R, alpha)` is exactly a parameterized interpolation between two lemma versors. The repo already performs this operation, at `packs/compiler.py:617` (`_blend_feature_versors(source, prototype, 0.40)`) — **in the opposite direction**, blending every substrate lemma toward the English prototype at mount time. **Reducible-to `rotor_power` ∘ `word_transition_rotor`.**
3. **Extensibility.** A lens expressed as a rotor over `(en_versor → substrate_versor)` with strength `alpha` would (a) make the substrate load-bearing, (b) make the FA-1 collapse *visible* rather than irrelevant, (c) subsume `_apply_mounted_primary_domain_resonance` as its `alpha = -0.40, prototype = en` special case, and (d) absorb the register axis's substantive arm (`chat/register_substantive.py`, ADR-0077 R6) as a second rotor on the same manifold. Full pairing in `22-consolidation-report.md`; the natural cluster is **A2.1 × A1 (Algebra & Geometry Foundations)**, which is `AA-84`'s pairing, confirmed here from the M4 end.

**Necessity/generality axis:** **generalization-candidate** (and, as built, **reducible-to a hardcoded dict** — see §3 sabotage level 1). The stated intent is irreducible and valuable; the shipped mechanism is neither.

## 3. Stack-level synthesis

### Internal consistency

**The arc is internally inconsistent at exactly one joint, and it is the load-bearing one.**

ADR-0073 defines anchor lens by a *contrast*: register varies the surface after the proposition is fixed; anchor lens varies the proposition before it is constructed. It states this three times (§Decision, §Backwards compatibility, §"Why this composes against register, not replaces it") and makes it the criterion of success ("not just marker variation").

ADR-0073a honors it exactly — the twelve authored lemmas exist precisely so a composer *could* pick a different one.

ADR-0073b honors it in its docstring (`loader.py:11–13`: lenses "parameterise the proposition-construction step at L1.3").

**ADR-0073c breaks it, silently.** §Surface lift replaces "pick which lemma to render" with "append `[lens(id):mode]` between the provenance tag and the period," calls the result *the surface lift*, and §Consequences then claims the umbrella's capability for it. No amendment, no supersession note, parent still `Accepted`.

ADR-0073d then weakens the *measurement* to match the weakened mechanism (three propositions → ≥2 distinct strings), again with no amendment. ADR-0074 inherits the weakened form at one further remove and calls the result orthogonality.

This is the `AA-13`/`AA-32` pattern from `21-drift-report.md` §2 — a child silently overriding a parent that still reads as governing — occurring **inside a single phased family, between phases ratified on the same day.** That is the sharpest instance of the pattern found so far, because the phased-family structure was specifically adopted ("Each sub-ADR ratifies its phase in isolation") to make exactly this kind of drift visible.

A second, smaller inconsistency: ADR-0073a specifies `en-collapse-<family>` as "a sentinel pointing at no real lexicon entry"; ADR-0073c's implementation materialized those sentinels into `en_collapse_anchors_v1`, a real mounted pack with weighted engagement edges — and did so in code comments only, with the pack's own manifest crediting a third ADR (0084) that does not mention it.
