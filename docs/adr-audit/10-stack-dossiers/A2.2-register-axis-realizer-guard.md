## 0. Why this is one stack

Six of the eight ADRs are one sequential build-out of a single idea, phased R1→R6 and explicitly cross-referencing each other by phase letter in every "Future ADRs unlocked" block:

- **R1 (0068)** — the data shape. A fourth pack class, sibling to identity/safety/ethics, that composes on the *surface* side rather than into `boundary_ids`. Ships deliberately dead: no runtime wiring, one architectural seam test.
- **R2 (0069)** — the wiring. `RuntimeConfig.register_pack_id`, a `RegisterPack` threaded to every composer, and three byte-identity invariants (A: `None` ≡ pre-R2; B: `None` ≡ `default_neutral_v1`; **C: `trace_hash` invariant under register**). Ships as a proven no-op.
- **R3 (0070)** — the first non-neutral pack (`terse_v1`), one knob (`disclosure_domain_count`), and the fourth invariant (`register_invariant_grounding`).
- **R4 (0071)** — deterministic *variation across turns*: a seeded selector over bounded discourse-marker buckets, plus `convivial_v1` and a `per_intent` schema widening.
- **R5 (0072)** — operator visibility: `TurnEvent.register_id` / `register_variant_id`, `core chat --register`, and `core demo register-tour` as a CI-falsifiable seam check.
- **R6 (0077)** — substantive consumption: terse actually compresses, convivial actually expands, a new `register_canonical_surface` layering boundary so those transforms cannot move `trace_hash`, and replacement of R5's weak tour gate.

**C1 (0075) and C2 (0076) are the coherence-guard pair spliced into the middle of that arc**, and they belong here for a reason ADR-0075 states outright: the register axis was the *instrument that exposed the defect*. Inspecting the R5 tour grid surfaced `"Light reveals truth, right?" → "Right does not thought."` — reproduced byte-identically under all three registers, which is correct register behavior (the axis preserves whatever the truth path produced) and therefore proof that the truth path itself was licensing illegal articulations. C1 builds the floor (a slot-type verifier at a single runtime seam); C2 fixes the specific input class upstream. ADR-0075's ordering argument is explicit: *"If R6 ships before C1, terse and convivial registers will start emitting substantively distinct surfaces on top of a path that can still produce word salad."* The two families are not adjacent by numbering accident — R6 is licensed by C1.

## 1. Stack-level claim

> **Register is a property of the realizer, not the proposition graph: a swappable, checksum-sealed presentation pack can vary what the user reads — depth, provenance disclosure, propositional expansion, discourse markers, turn-to-turn variation — while the grounding source, the chain resolution, and the `trace_hash` of the turn stay byte-identical; and a deterministic slot-type verifier at a single seam guarantees that whatever the register renders was a legal articulation to begin with.**

This is falsifiable, was pre-registered by ADR-0069 as **invariant C**, and is measurable today.

- **Pre-registered criterion (ADR-0069 §"The three byte-identity invariants", strengthened by ADR-0070's `register_invariant_grounding` and ADR-0077's `invariant_register_canonical_surface_constant_across_registers`):**
  `trace_hash(turn)` is invariant under `RuntimeConfig.register_pack_id` for every turn in the cognition lane, *while* the served surface is demonstrably non-trivially different across registers. Both halves are required — ADR-0077 exists precisely because the first half is trivially satisfiable by a register that does nothing.
- **Measurement performed / already available — two independent runs, both fresh at this SHA:**

  1. `python -m evals.register_tour.run_tour` at `cbfc8ccb`, exit 0, all five claims true:

     ```
     P1 'What is light?'
       default_neutral_v1  'Light is a visible medium that reveals truth. pack-grounded (en_core_cognition_v1).'
       terse_v1            'Light: visible medium that reveals truth.'

       trace_hash          97fe14ecd5e1…  ×3  (identical)
     P2 'Define knowledge.'   trace_hash 03521d6f8f68… ×3   surfaces differ ×3
     P3 'What is truth?'      trace_hash 7afe819684a3… ×3   surfaces differ ×3
     P4 'Light reveals truth, right?'  trace_hash 8f6408ea3024… ×3   terse ≡ neutral (see AA-A2.2-10)

     all_grounding_sources_identical          : True
     all_trace_hashes_identical               : True
     register_canonical_surfaces_identical    : True
     terse_substantively_differs              : True
     convivial_substantively_differs          : True
     ```

  2. `docs/research/register-matrix-truth-path-fix-2026-07-25.md` — the same invariant over **all 99 ratified register packs × the full cognition lane**: `801 passed, 0 failed` (17m50s), with `test_register_matrix_trace_hash_invariant` green across every register. Adopted, not re-run (17 minutes exceeds this audit's budget; the tour is the fast independent confirmation and it agrees).

- **Verdict: GO.** Invariant C holds, and it holds *non-trivially* — the surfaces genuinely diverge while the hash does not. This is one of the few claims in the corpus with both a pre-registered criterion and a measurement that clears it. **Two qualifications that do not overturn the verdict and are carried into §4:** (a) the claim's *enforcement site* has moved twice since ADR-0069 and now lives in a module no member ADR names (`AA-A2.2-2`); (b) the axis it protects was **completely dead on `main` for roughly two days** in July 2026 without any gate noticing, and invariant C stayed green throughout precisely *because* nothing was varying (`AA-A2.2-3`). A green invariant C is compatible with a nonexistent register axis; only the R6 substantive-distinctness claims discriminate, and they are existential (`AA-A2.2-10`).

1. **Necessity:** irreducible *as a class boundary*. ### Cumulative necessity/generality read

The stack introduces **one coherent generalizable mechanism** and **four narrow instances of it**.

The mechanism is: *a bounded, pack-declared, deterministic transform applied to a served surface after a canonical capture has been reserved for the hash.* ADR-0077's `apply_substantive_register` is its clean expression. But it exists in the tree as four separate constructions built in sequence:

| Instance | Site | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| `disclosure_domain_count` slice | inside `pack_grounded_surface` | R3 |
| seeded marker decoration | `chat/register_variation.py`, runtime seam | R4 |
| substantive boolean knobs | `chat/register_substantive.py`, runtime seam | R6 |
| `per_intent` dispatch | inside `pack_grounding`, unreachable | R4 |

**This is the Batch-1 B4 pattern, and the answer is worse than B4's.** The dossier's task was to check whether a later phase "quietly became a second independent mechanism instead of an extension." R6 did **not** — it is a clean extension and it correctly subsumes the axis. But **R3's knob never migrated into it**, so the stack now runs two mechanisms in parallel (in-composer slice, post-composer transform) for one category of thing, plus a third (`per_intent`) that is built and dead, plus a fourth (marker decoration) that is a genuinely different concern correctly separated. The consolidation target is unambiguous: `disclosure_domain_count` and `per_intent` should reduce into `apply_substantive_register`, leaving two mechanisms (substantive, decorative) where there are currently four.

Two further consolidation clusters visible only from stack level:

- **The pack-loader pattern** (`AA-A2.2-17`): register / anchor-lens / identity / safety / ethics loaders are five copies of one shape.
- **The tour-as-gate pattern** (`AA-A2.2-22`): register / anchor-lens / orthogonality / audit tours are four copies of one verification primitive — and one that has *demonstrably* caught a real regression, making it a high-value primitive to formalize rather than a low-value duplication to trim.
- **The discourse-marker dual** (`AA-A2.2-26`): C2 strips markers on the way in; R4 adds them on the way out. Two halves of one transform, in two modules, with no cross-reference.

### Blast radius if this stack's central claim is wrong

If invariant C failed — if register could move `trace_hash` — the cascade would be wide:

- **ADR-0073/0073b/0073c/0073d (anchor-lens family, Batch 2).** ADR-0077 §"Architectural constraint" states the two axes compose *because* one moves the hash and the other does not: "This invariant is the load-bearing reason the two axes compose without interference." Orthogonality-tour claims would need re-verdicting wholesale. **This is the largest single dependency and it is in the same batch.**
- **ADR-0074 (orthogonality tour)** — its entire premise.
- **ADR-0018 / ADR-0153 (trace and back-stamp)** — `compute_trace_hash`'s input set and the `TurnEvent` back-stamp would both need re-derivation; the standing audit-ledger **R7** item interacts.
- **ADR-0057 (teaching replay-equivalence gate)** — replay across sessions with different registers would stop being meaningful.
- **ADR-0086 / ADR-0087 (100-register catalog, rhetorical-style axis)** — 100 packs' worth of ratification would rest on a false premise.
- **MV / M4 layer cards** — `runtime_contracts.md`'s "`trace_hash` is not reconstructable from `TurnEvent`" contract is *derived from* invariant C; it would become a defect rather than a contract.
- **`wrong=0`** — indirectly. If presentation could move the truth identity, replay could not verify a served answer.

**It is not wrong.** Verified twice at this SHA. But `AA-A2.2-3` is the sharp edge: the invariant stayed green through two days in which the axis it protects did not exist, because *a dead axis trivially satisfies it*. The claim that actually needs guarding is the **conjunction** — hash constant **and** surfaces genuinely differ — and only ADR-0077's two existential gates test the second half, for two packs out of a hundred, on "at least one" prompt. `AA-A2.2-10` shows that gate already passing over a live no-op (P4). **The stack's real fragility is not invariant C; it is the falsifiability of the thing invariant C is paired with.**
