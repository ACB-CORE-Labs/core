## 0. Why this is one stack

Two ADRs written on the same day about different subsystems, joined by one thing: **neither adds a capability, and both work by converting a property that was already true into a CI-enforced pin.**

ADR-0051 is an audit pass over four mutable trust surfaces (`core pack validate` dynamic execution, language-pack loading, OOV grounding error messages, pack-mutation proposal-only enforcement). Three of the four were already correct; the ADR's contribution is to stop them being correct *by convention* and make them fail closed in CI. Its own words: "The ADR adds **no new capability**. It hardens what is already there and pins the trust boundary in regression tests."

ADR-0058 does the same move one layer up. ADR-0047 had already measured `forward_graph_constraint` to a null on the cognition lane; ADR-0058 declines to flip the default and promotes "we observed a null once" into "the null is a regression-tested invariant." Its own words: "promoting the null-lift observation from a historical A/B reading to a regression-tested invariant."

So the stack's real subject is not trust boundaries and it is not admissibility. **It is the pin as a governance instrument** — and the reason to audit these two together is that both pins have failed in the same direction, four months apart, for the same structural reason. ADR-0051's fence pin was *never written* and the property it would have guarded is now false. ADR-0058's pin *was* written, is green, runs in CI — and has lost the ability to detect the event it exists to detect. A pin that outlives its discriminating power is indistinguishable, from the outside, from a pin that works.

## 1. Stack-level claim

> A property that is currently true can be made durable by writing it down as a test; once pinned, the boundary or the null is safe from silent erosion.

ADR-0058's half of this is **falsifiable, pre-registered by the ADR itself**, and is treated here the way FA-1 treated ADR-0005/0015.

- **Pre-registered criterion** (ADR-0058 §Decision item 3, restated verbatim in `tests/test_forward_graph_constraint_null_lift.py:46-65`): on the cognition public split, `intent_accuracy` / `surface_groundedness` / `term_capture_rate` / `versor_closure_rate` are pair-wise identical between `forward_graph_constraint=False` and `=True`. Non-identity falsifies "engaged but inert."
- **Measurement performed** — fresh at `cbfc8ccb`, not adopted:
  - The two pinned tests: **2 passed**. All four watched metrics identical.
  - **Strengthened beyond the pin.** Running the same 13-case split under both configs and diffing `case_details` rather than aggregates: **13/13 served surfaces byte-identical, 13/13 `trace_hash` values identical, zero differing metric keys.** The constraint is not merely metric-inert; it is byte-inert on the served surface and hash-inert on the determinism spine.
  - **And the metrics have saturated.** ADR-0047 measured `intent_accuracy=100.0%`, `surface_groundedness=15.4%`, `term_capture_rate=0.0%`, `versor_closure_rate=100.0%` — two of four with headroom. At `cbfc8ccb` all four read **1.0**.
- **Verdict:** **GO on the claim, NO-GO on the instrument.** "Engaged but inert on today's cognition lane" is *more* true today than when it was written, and nothing has made the word "inert" stale. But the criterion is now **unfalsifiable in the direction it was built for**: with all four metrics at ceiling, a downstream lift cannot move them, so the test can only fail on a regression. ADR-0058 §Decision 3's stated purpose — *"if a downstream change in the realizer / surface-assembly layer ever moves a metric on the flag flip, this test fails and the architectural assumption … gets re-examined as a deliberate transition rather than silent drift"* — cannot be served by this test any more. The saturation was caused by the very work ADR-0058 credits on its own first page (ADR-0048–0053, plus the later climb from 91.7% to 100% `term_capture_rate`). **The ADR's closure mechanism was disabled by the success the ADR records.** (`AA-A2.3-1`.)

ADR-0051's half is not falsifiable in that sense — an audit pass has no measurable criterion — with one exception, which this audit did falsify: §Surface 3 asserts a specific, checkable proposition ("the test suite asserts it stays out of those paths transitively"). Both conjuncts are false at `cbfc8ccb` (§2, `AA-A2.3-2`).

#### 3. Liveness / integration

- **Surface 2 is the live one, and it is genuinely load-bearing.** `_validate_pack_id` runs on every `ChatRuntime` construction: `chat/runtime.py:635` `load_pack(mounted_pack_id)`, `:638` `load_pack_entries(...)`, `:640` `load_mounted_packs(pack_ids)` — all three of its wired entrypoints, in the constructor, on the default serving path.

Two tests fail immediately (`test_load_pack_rejects_traversal`, and `test_load_pack_does_not_touch_filesystem_for_bad_id` fails with the *diagnostic* `AssertionError: filesystem touched before pack_id validation`). This is not decoration; it is the only guard on that path, and before this ADR there was none.

- **Sabotage test — Surface 4.** Remove `frozen=True` and nothing *observable* changes today, because no caller mutates a proposal — but `tests/test_mutation_proposal_type.py` fails in four places. This is a type-level guard against a future refactor, which is exactly what the ADR says it is; it earns its keep as a tripwire, not as a runtime effect.
- **Surface 3 is live at sites the ADR did not name, and absent at the one it did.** `safe_display` is reached on the serving path through `chat/oov_surface.py:108` and `chat/partial_surface.py:104` — the cold-start OOV and partial-grounding surfaces built later under the ADR-0048…0066 arc — plus four `core/demos/*` adapters using `safe_pack_id`. - **The fence is breached, and it is reachable.** Measured by a module-level import-graph walk over 231 modules under `algebra/`, `generate/`, `field/`, `vault/`: one breach —
  `generate.grounding_accessors → chat.teaching_grounding → chat.pack_grounding → packs.register.loader:34 → core._safe_display`.

#### 5. Build fidelity — does the code match the decision?

Three divergences, in descending severity.

1. **The fence is asserted, unpinned, and false.** §Surface 3: "It must never be imported by `algebra/`, `generate/`, `field/`, or `vault/` — the module docstring states this **and the test suite asserts it stays out of those paths transitively**." The docstring half is true (`core/_safe_display.py:16-18`). The test half is **not built** — no import-graph test exists in `tests/` for this module — and the property it would have asserted is **false** at `cbfc8ccb` via `generate.grounding_accessors → chat.teaching_grounding → chat.pack_grounding → packs.register.loader:34 → core._safe_display` (§3). The ADR's own thesis is that convention-enforced properties erode and pinned ones do not; this is the clause where the ADR failed to take its own medicine, and the erosion happened exactly as it predicted for the unpinned case. `AA-A2.3-2`.
2. **The reserved follow-up never landed, and the reason the ADR gave for it being low-risk is no longer true.** §Follow-up: "Wiring `chat/runtime.py:_apply_oov_policy`'s `KeyError(f"OOV token requires vocab proposal: {token}")` to route through `core._safe_display.safe_display(token)`. The mechanical change is one line." Fifteen months on, `chat/runtime.py:1779` is unchanged. Worse, §Surface 3's mitigating premise — "The exception is raised at the runtime boundary and **is caught by `respond()`**" — is false today: `respond()` (`chat/runtime.py:3323-3331`) has no handler at all; it is `return self.chat(text, max_tokens=max_tokens).surface`. The raw user-controlled token now escapes uncaught to every caller of `respond()` and `chat()`. Same shape at `ingest/gate.py:235`, `:286` and `vocab/manifold.py:138,191,202,221,242`. `AA-A2.3-3`.
3. **Documentation drift, all in the growth direction.** Claimed test counts 20 / 24 / 9 are actually 21 / 33 / 9 — tests were added, none removed. `cmd_pack_validate` has moved from `core/cli.py` to `core/cli_pack.py` and now sources `_safe_pack_id` from a *third* location (`core/cli_teaching.py:1242`, byte-identical to `core/cli.py:601`), leaving the `core/cli.py` copy the ADR names as the audited one orphaned for this purpose. Behaviour is preserved throughout; a reader following the ADR's file references lands in the wrong file. `AA-A2.3-11`, `AA-A2.3-4`.

Everything else matches, including the parts that are easy to get wrong: the guard really does run before the first `Path` operation at all three entrypoints and there is a test that proves it *by making the filesystem explode*; the dry-run really does return before `importlib.util` is imported, not merely before `exec_module`; the error message really is routed through the sanitiser; and the six rejection classes are implemented in the stated order.

**Build-fidelity axis:** **partial drift** — the four audited surfaces match the decision exactly. The two clauses that drifted are both clauses the ADR wrote about *itself*: the test it said existed, and the follow-up it said was reserved.

#### 7. Necessity / generality

1. **Necessity.** Surface 2 is irreducible: it is the only guard between a caller-supplied string and a `Path` join in the language-pack loader, and it demonstrably did not exist before this ADR while its three siblings had one. Surface 1 is irreducible for the same reason (arbitrary code execution). Surface 4 is a tripwire with no runtime effect, which the ADR states plainly. Surface 3's *helper* is necessary as a concentration point — every site could inline an escape loop, but then there is no single place to audit and no single place to strengthen. The system would lose real capability without Surfaces 1–2.
2. **Reducibility.** Not reducible to L0/L1, and correctly so — this is a boundary concern and the ADR's own fence exists to keep it out of the algebra layer. There is no `algebra/` operator that sanitises strings and there should not be.
3. **Extensibility — and this is the finding.** `_validate_pack_id` is the **fifth** implementation of one concept in this repository:
   - `core/cli.py:601` `_safe_pack_id`
   - `core/cli_teaching.py:1242` `_safe_pack_id` — **byte-identical duplicate** of the above

## 3. Stack-level synthesis

### Internal consistency

The two members never reference each other and govern different subsystems, so there is no contradiction to find — and looking for one would be the wrong question. The consistency that matters is **methodological, and it is exact**: both ADRs are dated 2026-05-18, both add no capability, and both execute the same three-step move — *audit a property that is currently true → decline to change the thing → pin the property in CI so it cannot erode silently.* ADR-0051 §Context: "those rules have been enforced *by convention*… which means a future refactor could quietly weaken them." ADR-0058 §Why this is the right call: "Pinning the null-lift as CI-enforced turns 'observed once during ADR-0047' into 'structurally true across the codebase's evolution.'" Same sentence, different subject.

Both are also unusually well written for their genre. ADR-0058's title is a piece of vocabulary the rest of the corpus borrowed. ADR-0051's Surface 4 decision is the words "**Fix.** No code change — the type was already correct," which is what an audit pass should look like and rarely does.

### Cumulative build state

| ADR | Build | Liveness | Build fidelity | Necessity/generality | Fitness |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0051 | full | **live** (3 of 4 surfaces on the default path) | partial drift | generalization-candidate (5 copies of one guard) | positive, adopted at 6 later sites, unclaimed |
| 0058 | full | **live — executing, non-discriminating** | matches | irreducible (decision) / generalization-candidate (instrument) | decision valuable + precedent ×5; instrument measured inert |

**2 of 2 full; 2 of 2 live.** This is the healthiest cumulative build state of any Tier A stack so far in either batch — and it is precisely why the stack is worth reading as a unit, because the failure it exposes is invisible at the per-ADR level. Both ADRs built everything they promised (bar one test), both are live, and **both of their central pins have nonetheless stopped protecting what they were written to protect.** ADR-0051's fence pin was never written and its property is now false. ADR-0058's pin was written, is green, runs in CI — and cannot fail for the reason it exists.

The generalisation, stated once: **a pin is a claim with an expiry date, and nothing in this repository tracks the expiry.** A pin can stop working in three ways, and this stack contains all three:
1. **Never written** — ADR-0051's transitive import fence (`AA-A2.3-2`).
2. **Written, but the property it guards saturated** — ADR-0058's null-lift metrics (`AA-A2.3-1`).
3. **Written and green, but scoped to a population that stopped growing** — ADR-0058's one 13-case lane against a 129-lane corpus (`AA-A2.3-9`).
All three present as identical from the outside: a passing suite. That is the stack's transferable finding, and it applies well beyond these two ADRs — A4's `AA-88` (`passthrough_rate` computed over a deleted enum member) is mode 2 in a different zone, which makes this the **second stack to independently find a vacuous-metric pin**.

### Cumulative necessity/generality read

One coherent generalisable mechanism *per member*, and both are the same shape: **an N-th narrow instance of something that should exist once.**

- ADR-0051 shipped the **fifth** pack-id boundary guard (`AA-A2.3-4`) — two of the five byte-identical to each other — having explicitly noticed the other four. Feeder for `AA-111` and for Batch 2's own A2.4 stack (ADR-0091/0093, Domain Pack Contract Generalization), which is the natural adjudicator.
- ADR-0058 shipped the **first and only** flag-null-lift harness, hand-written for one of the **nine** default-off CAPABILITY flags in `flag_register.md` §3a/§3b. Its shape is already duplicated once in the repo, as ADR-0023's `_run_region_ablation`. Feeder for `22-consolidation-report.md` as a pairing with A4's evidence layer.

Both consolidations would pay twice: fewer copies, and — for the ADR-0058 case — a *better* instrument, since a parameterised harness comparing per-case surfaces and trace hashes (which this audit measured and found available, §2) does not saturate the way an aggregate rate does. `AA-A2.3-1` is fixed as a side effect of the consolidation, not as a separate repair.

### Blast radius if this stack's central claim is wrong

The central claim is ADR-0058's: `forward_graph_constraint` should stay off, and the null-lift is a stable, monitored state.

This is the scenario the charter's sabotage test exists to catch, and it is the one that obtains.
